# Fruit Merge provenance

Shipping authorization is outside this module. Discovery and this provenance record do not modify
the maintainer-owned production allowlist.

The implementation is classified as `new_expression`. Its Kotlin rules, fixed-step physics,
Compose UI, runtime Canvas fruit art, vector icon, procedural audio declarations, names, balance,
and interaction design are original to this repository. No source code, artwork, audio recordings,
music composition, fonts, or level data were copied.

The music and SFX use only the repository's public MiniApp procedural-audio API. Their 84 BPM
fruit-crate rhythm, seeded wooden knocks, rolling-noise layers, glass accents, and event-specific
drop/merge/clear/shake/game-over programs were authored for this game. Deterministic render tests
check finiteness, audibility, repeatability, and headroom. No third-party audio engine or asset is
part of the MiniApp.

Only the genre-level drop-and-merge rules were researched using:

- https://suikagame.jp/
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/suika-game-aladdin-x/id6469114836
- https://www.nintendo.com/au/games/nintendo-switch/suika-game/

OpenAI Codex assisted with research, design, and implementation. The reviewed prompt and product
decisions are archived in `../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-fruit-merge-design.md`.
The subsequent UX, onboarding, result-flow, performance, and audio decisions are archived in
`../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-30-fruit-merge-ux-polish-design.md`.
